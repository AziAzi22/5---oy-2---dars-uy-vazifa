This baby animal from India is the cutest in the world

Hint: It’s not as cuddly as you’d imagine
By Condé Nast Traveller
25 February 2024

Baby animals could have you saying ‘d’awww’ all day long. Now, a new study ranks the infant animals that humans love the most. Wildlife tour operators Explore Worldwide used eye-tracking technology to track which of the world’s baby animals caught the attention of the human eye the most.

How does it work?

The study analysed participants’ eye movements as they looked at images of 80 different baby animals from countries all around the world. The study tracked which species held the participants’ gaze the longest (fixation), and also which baby animal the participants kept returning to look at (number of fixations).

Which baby animals are the cutest?

As per the study, the Indian Rhinoceros topped the list, drawing a human’s gaze 104 times and holding it for 34 seconds. The largest population of the greater one-horned rhinoceros is found in India, with Kaziranga National Park in Assam being home to 70% of them.

The royal Bengal tiger ranked #14 on the list. The highest population of Bengal tigers in India reside in Madhya Pradesh. Morepork from New Zealand and Irish hare also ranked among the top three of the cutest baby animals from around the world. Scroll to see these baby animals that will definitely brighten up your day.
